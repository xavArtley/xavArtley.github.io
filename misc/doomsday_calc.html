Notes & Formulae
Century anchor formula

The century anchor for Gregorian dates is computed by anchor = (2 + 5 × (century mod 4)) mod 7. With 0=Sunday, this yields:

Odd+11 method (why it works)

It turns the last two digits y into a value congruent to -y modulo 7 by making it even, halving, repeating once, then taking the 7's complement. This gives the same offset as Conway's arithmetic but with mental-friendly steps.
